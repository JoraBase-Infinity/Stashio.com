# Poysha POS - Smart Shop Manager (Android)

Poysha POS is a full-featured, modern Point of Sale (POS) and retail management Android app rewritten in native Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Authentication & Security**: PIN-based account security (SHA-256 hashed), mobile verification, multi-user accounts.
- **Shop Management**: Multi-shop dashboard with instant switching between shops and locations.
- **Home Dashboard**:
  - Live greeting & date badge.
  - Real-time balances: Counter Cash, Today's Sales, Today's Expenses, Total Dues (Receive & Pay), Total Stock Qty.
  - Quick action buttons for **Sale** and **Purchase**.
- **Point of Sale (Sell)**:
  - Inventory search & barcode lookup.
  - Real-time cart calculations with customizable rates, quantities, and discounts.
  - Flexible payment options: Cash, bKash, Nagad, Bank, or Due (credit).
  - Instant professional printable invoices formatted with Bangladeshi Taka in words (`numberToWordsBDT`).
- **Purchase & Stock Restock**:
  - Supplier selection and restock logging.
  - Automatic inventory quantity replenishment and purchase bill records.
- **Ledger Books**:
  - **Sales Book**: Complete transaction history with invoice breakdown.
  - **Purchase Book**: Supplier purchasing records.
  - **Due Book**: Customer and supplier receivables/payables with direct collection/payment settlement dialog.
  - **Expense Book**: Categorized shop operating expenses.
- **Cash Box**:
  - Real-time counter cash ledger with Add Cash (In) and Withdraw Cash (Out) records.
- **Contacts & Parties**:
  - Filtered Customer and Supplier directory with one-tap phone calls and SMS messaging.
- **Business Reports**:
  - Estimated Gross Profit & Margin calculations (`Sales - Purchases - Expenses`).
- **Product Inventory**:
  - Add, edit, delete products with low-stock warnings and barcode support.
- **My Notes**:
  - Business reminders and follow-up notes.
